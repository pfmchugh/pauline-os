From this tool to your shirt
  1. Upload a simple, high-contrast PNG. Use the pale artwork guide to check letter centers and fine details.
  2. Choose the finished size, then adjust fill or outline and the gap. Thin strokes may need larger artwork.
  3. Download SVG and upload it into Cricut Design Space as a cut image. Keep all circles together using Attach if needed.
  4. Before cutting, check a circle is 3.43 mm (about 0.135 in) across. Avoid resizing the imported group: that changes the holes. Set a new size here and export again instead.
  5. Test a small patch on your flock with your stones before cutting the whole design. The SVG contains holes only, without a border or artwork guide.

PNG export is a visual preview, not a scaled cutting file. Outline rows follow outer and inner edges inward; tight areas may omit stones to maintain the gap. This tool does not mirror the artwork.
